# Creating an Account

> **Category:** User Guide | **Source:** [https://qniverse.in/docs/creating-an-account/](https://qniverse.in/docs/creating-an-account/)


**Step-by-Step Guide to Signing Up and Logging In**

**1\. Sign Up**

**Visit the[Qniverse Register](https://qniverse.in/register/) page** and **enter your details.**

**2\. Sign In**

**Visit the[Qniverse login](https://qniverse.in/login/) page** and **enter the Credentials.**

Upon successful login, you may visit the profile section to update your profile
