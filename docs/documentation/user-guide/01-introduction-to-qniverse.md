# Introduction to Qniverse

> **Category:** User Guide | **Source:** [https://qniverse.in/docs/introduction/](https://qniverse.in/docs/introduction/)


**Qniverse** is a dedicated platform that brings together quantum computing **researchers, students, and enthusiasts** across India. It serves as a hub for collaboration, learning, and exploration of cutting-edge advancements in quantum technology. Whether you are delving into quantum algorithms, developing quantum applications, or simply passionate about this transformative field, Qniverse provides resources and a community to support your journey. Qniverse aims to accelerate innovation and understanding in the quantum realm by fostering connections among professionals, academics, and curious minds.
